Some Else Where

Experience stories you can't experience.

A personal, single-channel automation tool that produces and publishes short-form video essays to its own YouTube channel, @someelsewhere.

What it does

  • Writes and renders short narrated video pieces.
  • Uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Reads that same channel's analytics to tune what it makes next.

Who can use it

It has exactly one user: its owner. It is not a product, a service, or an app other people sign into. It is run from the owner's own machine, against the owner's own channel.

Data

It does not collect, process, or share data belonging to anyone else. See the privacy policy.
